Standard Operating Protocols

All FEAR-ACU operatives are bound by the following containment protocols upon unit activation. Violation results in immediate suspension pending review.

α Perimeter Containment Protocol PRIORITY: CRITICAL

Upon receipt of activation signal, all field units establish a 500m-radius containment perimeter using established cordon methodology. Civilian evacuation initiated within 15 minutes. Authorized lethal force available only with explicit command authorization.

  • Secure ingress/egress routes — do not leave gaps
  • Issue public explanation: "Hazardous materials response" — deploy cover story
  • Document all personnel entering cordon zone
  • Establish visual isolation (thermal shielding, signal jamming where authorized)
  • Await FEAR-COMMAND clearance before escalation
β Information Control Protocol PRIORITY: HIGH

FEAR-ACU maintains a dedicated information operations sub-team at every activation. Their role: clean the digital and physical record. This section covers authorized methods of information control — and notes where those methods have been publicly observed.

  • Identify all observers within 1km radial; attempt voluntary removal via standard dispersal protocols
  • "Pending investigation" designated as default civilian-facing explanation
  • Media contact directed to Public Information Liaison (PIL) — internal designation only
  • Digital footprint removal via AGIS framework (for authorized platforms only)
  • Long-term NDA compliance program administered through private contractor
γ Civilian Extraction Protocol PRIORITY: STANDARD

Civilian extraction is the final and most delicate phase of FEAR-ACU response. Subject relocation is conducted under the guise of "protective custody" with full domestic legal compliance framework invoked retroactively where necessary.
